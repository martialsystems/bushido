Patterns: click the PATTERN screen or its key to pick one of 16 patterns (the last six are acid lines) (arrow keys and Enter work while the list is open). A pattern sets the knobs, switches and cables. BYPASS (top left): click OFF or ON; while it is on the sequencer keeps stepping but its gates are muted. Play: press START/STOP (its lamp lights while running), or Space. STEP and RESET work stopped or running. Knobs: drag up or down (Shift = fine), scroll, double-click resets. Click a half of a RANGE rocker for 1 V or 5 V. Click C MODE, SOURCE or MODE to switch. Cables: pick a color, then drag from a jack to another jack. Drag a plug to move it; drop it on empty space to unplug. Click a jack with cables to pick one from its stack or reorder it. Esc cancels. Patching works: TRIG N into INPUTS RESET makes an N−1 step loop, TRIG or GATE into STEP, START/STOP or CLOCK (SOURCE EXT) clock the sequencer, and CV into TEMPO CV bends the internal clock. Sound: two Hz/V VCOs listen to CV A / GATE A and CV B / GATE B (1 V = 55 Hz × the octave setting above). In C MODE CV, CV C opens both filters. The mixer is an audio utility with no audio source on this page.
