Patterns: two banks, A and B, of up to 999 patterns each; the lit lamp (A or B) is the bank you are browsing and saving to. Click the PATTERN screen or its key to open the list, then type to search (the screen shows what you type), use the arrow keys or scroll, and click a row or press Enter. A pattern sets the knobs, switches and cables. Bank A starts with the factory bank, INIT only for now. SAVE stores the panel as the next number in the lit bank: type a name and press Enter (or SAVE again). Saved patterns stay in this browser. BYPASS (top left): click OFF or ON; while it is on the sequencer keeps stepping but its gates are muted. Play: press START/STOP (its lamp lights while running), or Space. STEP and RESET work stopped or running. Knobs: drag up or down (Shift = fine), scroll, double-click resets. BPM: the green readout next to TEMPO is the same control in BPM; drag its digits (Shift = 0.1 BPM) or scroll. DIV sets the steps per beat (1/8, 1/16, 1/32): at 120 BPM and 1/16 the clock runs 8 steps per second. TEMPO CV still bends around it, and with SOURCE on EXT the readout shows EXT. Click a half of a RANGE rocker for 1 V or 5 V. Click C MODE, SOURCE or MODE to switch. Cables: pick a color, then drag from a jack to another jack. Drag a plug to move it; drop it on empty space to unplug. Click a jack with cables to pick one from its stack or reorder it. Esc cancels. Patching works: TRIG N into INPUTS RESET makes an N−1 step loop, TRIG or GATE into STEP, START/STOP or CLOCK (SOURCE EXT) clock the sequencer, and CV into TEMPO CV bends the internal clock. Sound: two VCOs listen to CV A / GATE A and CV B / GATE B under each row's PITCH LAW, by default the rack pitch standard, V/OCT with 0 V = C3 = 130.81 Hz (MIDI 48), at the footage set above (8' = C3). The sequencer is the plugin's engine, sample for sample. In C MODE CV, CV C opens both filters. The mixer is an audio utility with no audio source on this page.
