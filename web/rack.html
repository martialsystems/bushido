Two instruments, one patch: BUSHIDO (the step sequencer) and RONIN (the semi-modular synth), both from the Martial Systems Jidai Collection, share one patch graph and one set of cables. Patch either way: BUSHIDO's CV and gates into RONIN, and RONIN's outputs back into BUSHIDO's CLOCK, TEMPO CV, START/STOP, STEP, RESET and MIXER inputs (an MG PULSE into CLOCK with SOURCE on EXT clocks the sequencer). On top picks which panel sits above; the one below gets a shadow along its top edge, and cables stay on their jacks. The graph follows RONIN's rules: Audio or CV into a Gate input is refused, a GATE or TRIG into a RONIN CV input arrives as an S-trig (0 V while high), and in a feedback loop the newest cable is delayed one sample. CV A into VCO HZ/V plays RONIN's VCO on the Hz/V law. Play RONIN's VCO sets that up for you: CV A to HZ/V, GATE A to EG 1 TRIG, VCO through VCF and VCA 1. By default BUSHIDO's own two VCOs play into RONIN's EXT IN, the way a track feeds an effect in a DAW, so RONIN's presets (03 VOICE is the default) process them. Choose "straight out" to hear them dry, or "off". RONIN presets: the same screen as BUSHIDO's: banks A and B of up to 999, click the PRESET screen or its key, type to search, arrows or scroll, Enter or click; SAVE stores RONIN's knobs, Effect switch and own cables as the next number in the lit bank. Bank A holds the 13 factory presets (A008 to A013 are the self-modulating patches, which play with no HOLD press). A factory preset sets RONIN's knobs and RONIN-only cables and leaves cables to BUSHIDO alone. Press and hold HOLD to open the EXT IN gate. The rocker is the plugin's Effect switch: off passes OUTPUT L/R dry, on blends in WET by MIX. Click a RONIN jack to point the meter at it (±5 V full scale); it starts on OUTPUT WET. VCA 1/2 INITIAL and MOD, the DIV RATIO SWITCH and the DIV /16 jack are not wired in this page's RONIN build yet.
Rack patches (bank B on both screens): B001 LOOP BASS (08 FILTER LOOP, the filter re-driving its own cutoff, with BUSHIDO's row C pushing the loop) and B002 RING SEED (13 SELF RING, woken by BUSHIDO's row C summed into RING B). Loading one from either screen sets both instruments and every cable, then press START. Details in sequenced_selfmod/README.md. BUSHIDO patterns: two banks, A and B, of up to 999 patterns each (bank B starts with the two rack patches); the lit lamp is the bank you are browsing and saving to. Click the PATTERN screen or its key to open the list, type to search, use the arrows or scroll, then click a row or press Enter. A pattern sets BUSHIDO's knobs, switches and its own cables. Bank A has 28 factory patterns (A011 to A016 acid, A017 to A028 90s scenes). SAVE stores the panel as the next number in the lit bank (type a name, then Enter). Saved patterns stay in this browser. BYPASS on keeps BUSHIDO stepping with its gates muted. Play: START/STOP (or Space), STEP and RESET. Knobs: drag up or down (Shift = fine), scroll, double-click resets. BPM: drag the green digits next to TEMPO; DIV sets steps per beat (120 BPM at 1/16 is 8 steps per second). Cables: pick a color, then drag from a jack to another jack. Drag a plug to move it; drop it on empty space to unplug. Click a jack with cables to pick one from its stack or reorder it. Esc cancels.
