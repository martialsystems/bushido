Two instruments, one patch: BUSHIDO (the SQ-10 sequencer) sits above RONIN (the MS-50 synth from the MS50Modular repo). They share one patch graph and one set of cables, so you can patch any BUSHIDO jack to any RONIN jack. The graph follows the MS-50's rules: Audio or CV into a Gate input is refused, a GATE or TRIG into a RONIN CV input arrives as an S-trig (0 V while high), and in a feedback loop the newest cable is delayed one sample. CV A into VCO HZ/V plays RONIN's VCO on the Hz/V law. Play RONIN's VCO sets that up for you: CV A to HZ/V, GATE A to EG 1 TRIG, VCO through VCF and VCA 1. By default BUSHIDO's own two VCOs play into RONIN's EXT IN, the way a track feeds an effect in a DAW, so RONIN's presets (03 VOICE is the default) process them. Choose "straight out" to hear them dry, or "off". RONIN: click the screen or its key for the 7 factory presets. A preset sets RONIN's knobs and RONIN-only cables and leaves cables to BUSHIDO alone. Press and hold HOLD to open the EXT IN gate. The rocker is the plugin's Effect switch: off passes OUTPUT L/R dry, on blends in WET by MIX. Click a RONIN jack to point the meter at it (±5 V full scale); it starts on OUTPUT WET. VCA 1/2 INITIAL and MOD, the DIV RATIO SWITCH and the DIV /16 jack are not wired in the plugin either. BUSHIDO patterns: two banks, A and B, of up to 999 patterns each; the lit lamp is the bank you are browsing and saving to. Click the PATTERN screen or its key to open the list, type to search, use the arrows or scroll, then click a row or press Enter. A pattern sets BUSHIDO's knobs, switches and its own cables. Bank A has 28 factory patterns (A011 to A016 acid, A017 to A028 90s scenes). SAVE stores the panel as the next number in the lit bank (type a name, then Enter). Saved patterns stay in this browser. BYPASS on keeps BUSHIDO stepping with its gates muted. Play: START/STOP (or Space), STEP and RESET. Knobs: drag up or down (Shift = fine), scroll, double-click resets. BPM: drag the green digits next to TEMPO; DIV sets steps per beat (120 BPM at 1/16 is 8 steps per second). Cables: pick a color, then drag from a jack to another jack. Drag a plug to move it; drop it on empty space to unplug. Click a jack with cables to pick one from its stack or reorder it. Esc cancels.
